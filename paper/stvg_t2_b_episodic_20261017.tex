\begin{abstract}
Spatio-temporal video grounding requires both an event interval and the trajectory of a language-specified instance. We adapt a pretrained grounding model using frozen language--video feedback, without target annotations. A two-candidate temporal branch compares a Native interval with a distinct alternative selected by frozen time scores; Caption--Query consistency supervises a 512-parameter update while full-grid distribution preservation retains the original temporal support. The resulting interval determines sparse spatial observations. A fixed Query--Caption evidence policy allocates a compact visual budget, and actual Teacher-selected candidate boxes reward on-policy Gaussian actions. A 1,792-parameter spatial interface realizes these preferences through the existing response-filtered update and complete decoded acceptance. The method resets to Source for every query. Its optimization is organized as evidence-conditioned distribution improvement followed by restricted parameter realization. We evaluate the temporal and spatial branches separately, distinguish observation conditioning from metric coupling, and retain the previously completed negative exploration controls. The integrated two-candidate Full evaluation is being executed under a frozen manifest; the manuscript does not substitute historical four-candidate results for that evaluation.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
A grounding model can identify when an event occurs while assigning its description to an incorrect instance, or track an object accurately outside the relevant event. Adapting only a single output therefore leaves a structural gap in spatio-temporal grounding. Our setting permits a frozen language--video Teacher to evaluate incoming unlabeled evidence while a pretrained Student supplies the complete temporal grid and spatial trajectory.

We organize adaptation around two restricted interfaces. Caption--Query feedback adjusts a small temporal readout, and sparse candidate selections supervise the spatial decoder. The two branches share an output-distribution improvement principle, but their observations, rewards and parameterizations remain distinct. This organization makes it possible to measure a branch's independent contribution and to ask whether a corrected observation interval improves spatial supervision.

Our method uses a fixed two-candidate temporal design and a fixed spatial evidence policy rather than a search over mechanisms at evaluation time. The temporal choice simplifies the tested four-candidate design; it is a declared design decision and does not assert that statistical noninferiority has already been established. The spatial branch retains the original sparse Gaussian OPD and response filter. The implementation manifest, complete branch controls and saved negative exploration results specify the scope of the empirical claims.

\section{Method}
\subsection{Setting and lifecycle}
For video $V$ and query $Q$, a frozen Source Student returns a Native interval $I_N$ and boxes $b_0(t)$ on its native physical-frame grid. Every query begins with the same Source parameters, fresh optimizer state and deterministic query-bound sampling. Temporal adaptation produces $I_T$; spatial adaptation produces the complete trajectory $b_\phi(t)$. The final tube uses $(I_T,b_\phi)$. There is no persistent cross-query LN writeback. Query and Caption strings are input data, never executable instructions; annotations are absent from evidence construction, updating and acceptance.

\subsection{Evidence-conditioned distribution improvement}
Given a fixed reference policy $p_0$ and reward $R$, consider
\begin{equation}
q^*=\arg\max_q\{\mathbb E_q[R]-\tau\KL(q\|p_0)\},\qquad \tau>0.
\end{equation}
Normalization of $q$ gives
\begin{equation}
q^*(y)=\frac{p_0(y)\exp(R(y)/\tau)}{\int p_0(y')\exp(R(y')/\tau)\,dy'}.
\label{eq:improvement}
\end{equation}
This relative-entropy regularization is an established optimization construction, including in REPS~\cite{reps}. We use its structure for discrete temporal hypotheses and continuous spatial actions. The two branches do not thereby form a calibrated joint posterior or share a guarantee about object identity.

\subsection{Two-candidate Caption--Query temporal adaptation}
The first hypothesis is $I_N$. The second is a legal union of one interval from each original frozen offset grid, ranked by the sum of their frozen scores, distinct from $I_N$. Ties follow original grid-row order, and each candidate must admit eight distinct legal physical frames. If no distinct legal alternative exists, the branch keeps Native without an update. No Caption reward or GT chooses this alternative, and no hand-expanded or contracted candidate is added.

A frozen Teacher describes each candidate clip and evaluates support, contradiction and uncertainty between the description and the same query. Its reward is $R_T=p(\mathrm{support})-p(\mathrm{contradiction})$, the expected utility of labels with utilities $(1,-1,0)$. These label probabilities are not assumed calibrated. The fixed initial score includes the existing actionness projection, median/MAD normalization and Native-prior fusion. We treat it as an energy $s_0$, so $p_0\propto\exp(s_0)$, and retain the registered coefficients.

Temporal-Lite adjusts affine logits with $\theta\in\mathbb R^{512}$. On the shared candidate distribution and each full legal offset grid $j$,
\begin{equation}
s_{\theta,j}(I)=s_{0,j}(I)+\varphi_j(I)^\top\theta.
\end{equation}
The fixed candidate target $q_T$ follows Eq.~\eqref{eq:improvement}. With $O$ offsets, the actual objective is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal L_T(\theta)=O\KL(q_T\|p^C_\theta)
 +\lambda_G\sum_{j=1}^{O}\KL(p_{0,j}\|p_{\theta,j}),\quad \lambda_G=1.
\end{equation}
The candidate logits use the existing shared mean of offset scores/features. Optimization retains the original deterministic L-BFGS/Armijo procedure, zero initialization and zero parameter L2. The final interval is decoded on the complete original time grid, rather than selected from only the two Teacher clips.

\paragraph{Conditional convexity.}
For fixed rewards, supports and frozen features, each forward-KL term is a log-sum-exp of affine logits minus a linear term. Consequently,
\begin{equation}
\nabla^2\mathcal L_T=O\operatorname{Cov}_{p^C_\theta}[\varphi_C]
 +\lambda_G\sum_j\operatorname{Cov}_{p_{\theta,j}}[\varphi_j]\succeq0.
\end{equation}
Thus the conditional objective is convex. This does not imply strict convexity, a unique parameter vector, finite-step exact convergence or correct Teacher rewards.

\subsection{Fixed B spatial evidence}
Four unique observations in $I_T$ define the context and local candidate clips. We keep the existing prospective E1 candidate construction, candidate IDs, source-candidate exclusion and sparse-reference admission. The frozen R1 Teacher sees four context frames and a five-frame local candidate clip, with global/local visual keep budgets of64/96 and96 output tokens. The existing fractional-uniform time encoding is retained.

Caption acquisition is independent of the old four-candidate eligibility gate. Among available two-candidate Captions, the original greatest-interval-overlap rule selects the description, with canonical-ID tie breaks; if absent, a legal Native description is acquired independently. Query and Caption embedding ranks $r_i^Q,r_i^C$ determine the fixed B score:
\begin{align}
s_i&=.75\min(r_i^Q,r_i^C)+.25|r_i^Q-r_i^C|\\
&=.75\bar r_i-.125\Delta_i,\quad
\bar r_i=(r_i^Q+r_i^C)/2,\quad\Delta_i=|r_i^Q-r_i^C|.
\end{align}
At equal average relevance the score penalizes disagreement. It has the exact normalized robust interpretation
\begin{equation}
\frac{s_i}{.75}=\min_{\alpha\in[1/3,2/3]}\{\alpha r_i^Q+(1-\alpha)r_i^C\}.
\end{equation}
The uncertainty interval remains a fixed design assumption on comparable ranks, not a learned reliability interval or a correctness probability. The original scale is retained inside MMR; rescaling only $s_i$ would alter its diversity tradeoff.

The registered policy also preserves ID and context tokens, the$.7/.3$ frame relevance/change allocation, the$.8$ object-share rule and the candidate-priority coverage loop. Their complete implementation and coefficients are frozen in the manifest. B is a fixed evidence allocation rule, not a claim that every component or B as a whole universally outperforms A. Only actual admitted Teacher-selected boxes become pseudo-references; the full trajectory is not fabricated by interpolation of these references.

\subsection{Sparse Gaussian OPD and parameter realization}
At active anchor $a$, the current Student mean is $\mu_a(\phi)=\operatorname{logit}(b_\phi(a))$ in logit-cxcywh coordinates. We sample32 antithetic actions using the fixed domain-specific exploration scale:
\begin{equation}
z_{am}=\mu_a(\phi_k)+\sigma\epsilon_{am},\qquad
w_{am}=\operatorname{softmax}_m\!\left(\frac{\operatorname{IoU}(\operatorname{sigmoid}(z_{am}),b_a^T)}{\tau_S}\right).
\end{equation}
Actions and normalized weights are detached. With these samples fixed, the Gaussian likelihood objective is exactly, up to a parameter-independent constant,
\begin{equation}
\mathcal L_S(\phi)=\frac{1}{2\sigma^2|\mathcal A|}\sum_{a\in\mathcal A}
\left\|\mu_a(\phi)-\sg\!\left[\sum_m w_{am}z_{am}\right]\right\|^2+C.
\end{equation}
It asks the shared Student parameters to realize a reward-weighted output target. The Student mapping is nonlinear, so the parameter objective is not asserted convex. The32-sample self-normalized approximation is not an unbiased exact posterior estimator.

The actual update retains the existing Adam proposal $d$ and response filter
\begin{equation}
\delta=(I+\lambda J_U^\top J_U)^{-1}d,\quad
\lambda=\mu_R\frac{\|d\|^2}{\|J_Ud\|^2},\qquad\mu_R=.125.
\end{equation}
Here $J_U$ differentiates normalized \emph{box-coordinate} outputs on the original unobserved support; it is not the logit Jacobian of the OPD target. For a fixed nonnegative $\lambda$, this is a quadratic proximity-to-$d$ problem with response penalty, solved by the original bounded CG. The norm-ratio coefficient sets relative protection strength and is not a solved KL-budget dual or a guarantee on nonlinear output displacement.

Every fullstep proposal is completely decoded. Finite outputs and nondecreasing mean IoU against the same sparse pseudo-references, within the original numerical tolerance, determine acceptance. Rejection restores parameters and optimizer state and retains the original early stop. The final readout recomputes all affected Decoder/LN paths and gates over every output frame; an immutable frozen prefix may be exactly reused. No reference-free query is dropped: it keeps Source spatial predictions. There is no new annealing, dense Tube target or Decoder-Aligned constrained target solver in this version.

\section{Experimental protocol and current evidence}
The integration cohort contains256 previously exposed independent parents, HC128 and Vid128. Frozen, Temporal-only, true Native-clock Spatial-only and Full form the main branch table. Only the new Full uses newly executed T2-conditioned observations and references; old T4 Full is an explicit historical control. Caption/input/Source equality, not a familiar alias, governs reuse. All new predictions and root checks are sealed before evaluation GT is read. Unknown supports, no-reference sources and signed per-parent outcomes remain in their denominators.

We report tIoU, dense and support-specific sIoU, vIoU, recalls at$.3/.5$, paired per-parent intervals and full signed outcomes. Spatial-only must preserve Native tIoU, and Full must preserve T2-only tIoU. A shared known-GT geometric support and fixed-final-time evidence comparison separate spatial changes from interval-dependent metric effects. Observation conditioning is not presumed beneficial in both domains or identified as gradient synergy by vIoU interaction alone.

The completed T2/T4 ablation found T2-minus-T4 vIoU changes of$+.0387/-.0421$pp on HC/Vid and did not establish the prelocked noninferiority criterion. The declared preference for T2 favors the simpler candidate set. Logical candidate pairs fell from884 to512; this is not a42\% measured end-to-end speedup. Existing A/B/C, noCoverage, noise-order and deterministic Tube-completion controls retain their original cohorts and negative findings in the accompanying reports, rather than being pooled into one cross-cohort ranking.

Official full-roster evaluation, a scientifically qualified second Backbone adapter and matched end-to-end cost measurement evaluate this same frozen method; they do not authorize further model selection. The existing TubeDETR interface probes alone are not evidence of scientific transfer. Independent model-assisted semantic review is labeled separately from human annotation and remains partial where pixels have not been reviewed.
