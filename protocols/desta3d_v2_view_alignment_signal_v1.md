alignment can still drive a nonzero source feature update. Such a gradient
is not evidence of task harm. Source samples are not a target population.

direction, a single matched identity-view ablation can distinguish augmentation
bias; it must be separately registered before target inference. If neither is
supported, keep competing readout and weak-evidence explanations. Do not infer
task-gradient conflict without task supervision, and do not choose steps by
loss. No gamma/LR/weight grid, no fresh teacher, no64 expansion or promotion.
