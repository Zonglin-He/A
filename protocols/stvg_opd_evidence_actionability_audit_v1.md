This is one finite CPU diagnostic on the original P7 clean roster: 128 historical nondevelopment parents in HC and 128 in VidSTG, the two original orders, and both original OPD updates Omega0 and Omega1. All 512 cells and 1,024 saved updates are retained. Prior P6 32, added96, all128 and order1/order2 are reported separately. No fresh confirmation set, model forward, GPU fit, expert call, optimizer update, gate training, threshold fitting, dynamic writeback or production promotion is authorized by this diagnostic. The active C expanded-state run and all its scientific pins remain unchanged. Previously cancelled remaining formal baselines remain cancelled.

Before reading GT, seal the complete new cache readouts and every test-visible signal. Verify original global barriers, original full fit/input hashes, P7 expert hashes, identical physical frames/pixels/frozen source, the exact common Before boxes and initial parameter state, and original accepted Top1 admission. Old mathematics/state qualification is reused, not called a new model/Jacobian replay. Stored arrays are physical real decoder boxes, not interpolated extra observations.

For an update trained on Omega0, held-out evidence consists of admitted Omega1 anchors whose physical frames were not actually observed by Omega0. Reverse the roles for Omega1. Rejected proposals are not admitted by the diagnostic. Agreement is unweighted geometric box IoU at those original real frames, with the original HC lower-coordinate clipping convention. The held-out score is after-minus-before agreement. It uses all eligible held-out anchors without GT-event filtering. The same DINO generates both sets; this is cross-frame evidence, not an independent teacher or an identity oracle. Empty held-out sets stay undefined and remain in coverage counts.

Six fixed test-visible scores are compared: held-out agreement gain; mean admitted detector score; Before agreement on training anchors; training agreement gain; pairwise geometric consistency of training anchors; and negative mean normalized decoder-output displacement (smaller displacement has the predeclared safe-update polarity). No score polarity is flipped after looking at GT. Expert GT IoU is an additional offline oracle diagnostic and is never described as test-visible.

The requested per-update actionability label uses the mean after-minus-before spatial IoU on GT-annotated event frames among actual decoder frames excluding that update's observed positions (own-U). A stricter primary readout uses common-U, excluding both observation sets, so held-out positions cannot also provide the GT outcome being predicted. Missing GT, unsupported frames and interpolation are not converted to zero or treated as real decoder frames. Positive, negative, exact-neutral (tolerance1e-12), undefined and severe <-5pp/<-20pp outcomes are all retained.

Teacher quality is mean IoU of admitted training boxes on available GT event annotations. Mean quality>=0.5 is a prespecified descriptive geometric-accuracy threshold, not an online gate. Report a stricter fully-labeled all-anchors>=0.5 definition, annotation coverage, unknown cases, and both-order repeated harmful cases. Neither definition proves referent identity or causal attribution to Adam/LN/Jacobian mechanisms.

Primary source statistics average each parent's two orders first, then rank parent means. Each single order is also evaluated. AUROC and tie-group average precision (AP, not trapezoidal PR area) exclude neutral/undefined outcomes only from the binary estimand while retaining their counts. Report eligible positive prevalence, signal coverage, all-parent outcome proportions, 10,000 paired parent bootstrap intervals (seed20261012), and undefined resamples with missing classes. Compare held-out AUROC to every simple signal on their identical intersection; do not compare mismatched eligible subsets. No pooling of two orders or scopes as independent videos.
