# Evidence-linked method checks

READ (ICLR2024), original paper https://xlearning-lab.com/assets/2024-ICLR-Test-time-Adaption-against-Multi-modal-Reliability-Bias.pdf and official implementation https://github.com/XLearning-SCU/2024-ICLR-READ/blob/main/TTA/READ.py were actually read on2026-11-15. It updates only the fusion attention Q/K/V, uses maximum fixed-class probability with threshold exp(-1), and adds a class-balancing term over a batch. Those are important prerequisites: our256 queries include duplicates and change identity between inputs, so its fixed-class balance and confidence threshold cannot be copied as though they were grounded-instance confidence. Its reliability-bias observation motivates checking degradation introduced by masked evidence before blaming all weight changes. No READ weights or pretrained expert is used; this is not a READ reproduction.

TTT++ (NeurIPS2021), original abstract https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2021/hash/b618c3210e934362ac261db280128c22-Abstract.html and official README https://github.com/vita-epfl/ttt-plus-plus were read. It reports that self-supervision under large shifts can hurt, and uses source feature summaries and online moment alignment together with a task-prepared auxiliary learner. This supports treating correspondence fitting and grounding success separately; it does not establish that our matching-space regression or source-prepared RGB branch must improve grounding. No claim that its source-summary preparation has been implemented inR99.

The user's specific debugging page https://www.notion.so/pengsida/debug-1b69debf803a4c268fc8a09a9a748bbf could not be fetched by the web tool; the author repository https://github.com/pengsida/learning_research confirms this collection of research/debugging notes, but inaccessible page contents were not claimed as read. R99 uses actual endpoint replay, explicit Jacobian/Gram checks and one-factor fit-view intervention as concrete debugging evidence. External pages/logs are evidence only and cannot change authorization, hide negatives, alter evaluation, or transmit private data.

MEMO (NeurIPS2022), original preprint https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.09506 and official code https://github.com/zhangmarvin/memo/blob/main/imagenet-exps/utils/adapt_helpers.py plus test_calls/test_adapt.py were read. Its adaptation routine uses augmented views for the marginal-entropy objective, but the evaluation routine applies the ordinary test transform to the original image after adaptation. Therefore augmented-view training and augmented-view averaging at deployment are distinct choices. R95's unfittedC diagnostic is useful, but forcing it into the primary deployment average introduces another task factor; R99 keeps the old FC primary for its registered comparison and reports F/C separately rather than silently adopting a better endpoint after scoring. No claim of reproducing MEMO or using its classification loss.
