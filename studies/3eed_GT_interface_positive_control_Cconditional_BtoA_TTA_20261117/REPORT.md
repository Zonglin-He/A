## One unlabeled repair, fixed before GT-control outcomes

Replace F-reversal AB-unanimity hinge with C-conditional B group-MAX distillation:

\[L=L_{partial}(S,C)+\mathrm{KL}(q_B^C\Vert\operatorname{softmax}(\max_{i\in g}z_\theta(i))_{g\in C})+L_{featurekeep}.\]

All weights1; original score-unit temperature1. The teacher is frozen sourceB, not a new pretrained expert or GT. B may disagree withA; distribution is soft, not a unique target label. S/C remain the exact preexisting physical qualifications and union log-gap set. Unknown groups outsideC do not participate in rankKL; final selection still considers allA256. This explicitly supplies relative information withinC, but does not certify teacher correctness.

Wrong control permutes target probabilities only withinC/equal group-size bins, preserving support, target probability multiset/entropy and update budget. Exact direct control first applies oldpartial direct, then matches the same C-group-max distribution while preserving each group internal query ratios and Cquerymass; outsideC afterpartial is unchanged. Existing fullFrozenF,ABmean,Bteacher,R135A/F/wrong are retained. These are internal adapted controls, not official published baselines.

## GT target scope versus C-qualified teacher

The oracle optimizes global A256 GT-best candidates, whereas the unlabeled objective only ranks C; they do not have equal supervision scope. Of764 registered inputs,453 retain a global GT-best candidate inC and311 do not. With the original full-cohort denominator, these strata contribute respectively +0.875654/+0.398106pp toGT3, versus +0.160750/-0.095370pp toC_B_A. A partial teacher both misses correct candidates and gives weak withinC ordering; the positive control must not be interpreted as a pure matched-target loss comparison. In423 recoverable wrong-A cases, new noGT updates improve the best-good margin211 times and worsen it212 times. Existing R135 first-gradient common nonzero-task subset(n594) has GTcosine mean-0.03435, versus +0.03407 forC_B_A, but this modest alignment improvement does not beat direct usage. No GT strata are used as deployed gates.
