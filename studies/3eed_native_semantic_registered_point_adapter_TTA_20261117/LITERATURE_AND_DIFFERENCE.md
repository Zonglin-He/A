# Relation to earlier experiments

R57's actual REPORT and implementation used independent samples of one physical cloud, approximate support/IoU transport between differing candidate sets, and the complete 184960-parameter matching MLP. Frozen/EMA soft targets and head-only fitting did not robustly beat equal frozen sampling aggregation. R75's numerics75.py used complete-reference query/token row and column KL on masked-context query features, fixed reference text projection and the same complete matching MLP; it also failed its same-information frozen reader comparison. R58 added loss-decreasing step acceptance, without establishing task correctness.

R125 does not claim that KL or backtracking is a new principle. The evidence motivating a bounded re-test is specific: R123 uses actual registered observations at fixed canonical spatial query anchors; its pointInfoNCE at the pre-language Adapter failed grounding despite auxiliary improvements. R124 source-only controlled GT interventions showed that exactly that 4896-parameter pointUp interface and fixed three-step budget can affect task decisions. No R124 labels, gradient vectors or optimized states are used in R125.

The minimal new hypothesis is task-native agreement between actual registered A/B grounding distributions at this interface. It is still self-distillation and can reinforce shared errors. Same-information direct probability consensus, stronger frozen mean/headmean, free feature-variable optimization, wrong-binding and norm-matched random updates are therefore mandatory. C is not decoded until proposals are fixed. Canonical slot preservation prevents accidental random query-ID equivalence, but neither registration nor consensus certifies object identity. Objective decrease or cross-view agreement is not localization success.

PointContrast's original/official conditions and why they do not guarantee short-step grounding TTA are recorded in the published R123 study. This follow-up does not add a paper-derived theorem, source preparation, expert, visual semantic label or unverified literature claim. A failure ends this finite recipe rather than licensing another step/layer/temperature sweep.
