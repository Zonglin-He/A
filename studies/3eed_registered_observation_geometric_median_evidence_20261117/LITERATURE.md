# Mechanism sources and applicability boundary

Pillutla, Kakade and Harchaoui, *Robust Aggregation for Federated Learning*, IEEE TSP2022: [author paper](https://krishnap25.github.io/papers/2019_rfa.pdf), [official repository](https://github.com/krishnap25/RFA), [author geometric-median implementation](https://github.com/krishnap25/geom_median), [official TFF implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google-research/federated/master/robust_aggregation/robust_federated_aggregation.py).

The paper replaces averaging of client updates with a geometric-median aggregate. Its smoothed Weiszfeld procedure repeatedly recomputes inverse-distance weights. Official TFF defaults to five communication passes and denominator floor1e-6; the author's independent NumPy implementation allows100iterations. These are computation settings for that objective, not grounding hyperparameters. We read those files as evidence, did not install/execute their setup instructions or copy third-party snapshots into the public study.

R122 borrows only robust aggregation. A seed has at most three valid aligned observation vectors. Its Euclidean geometric median lies in their triangle; we use the exact three-point Fermat solution instead of choosing an iteration count. For an angle at least120degrees the corresponding vertex is optimal; otherwise barycentric coordinates follow the first Fermat point. [Triangle-center author's coordinates](https://faculty.evansville.edu/ck6/tcenters/trilin.html), [geometric median triangle result](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/45525?nt=1). We separately check convex optimality/KKT on actual cached vectors and compare objective to the mean.

This is NOT an RFA reproduction, no federated clients or new pretrained model, and no claim to grounding novelty. Physically supported observations may still be correlated, mismatched, or contaminated by context. We have not established a majority of semantically correct features. Median aggregation can discard a genuinely useful minority observation. Correct geometry does not certify object identity; reduced aggregation objective does not certify task improvement. Fixed/global/shuffled weights and wrong correspondences therefore remain essential controls. The experiment is a direct-use evidence prerequisite, not a claim of parameter-TTA gain.
