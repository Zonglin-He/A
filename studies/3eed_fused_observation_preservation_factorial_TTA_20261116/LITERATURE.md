# Output preservation is an existing mechanism, not a new claim

[Learning without Forgetting, ECCV 2016](https://github.com/lizhitwo/LearningWithoutForgetting) preserves previous-network responses while acquiring new task capabilities without the old training inputs. Its [official distribution-loss implementation](https://github.com/lizhitwo/LearningWithoutForgetting/blob/release/vl_nnsoftmaxdiff.m) supports multiple distribution distances and temperature-based distillation. This supports output preservation as a general mechanism, not correctness of any grounding pseudo-target.

[EcoTTA, CVPR 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.01904) uses lightweight adaptation networks and self-distilled regularization relative to a frozen original network. The inspected abstract supports limiting deviation to preserve learned knowledge; the CVF page returned403, so no uninspected formula is asserted. Its architecture/training conditions differ from this fixed-checkpoint experiment. We introduce no new meta-network and claim no reproduction.

R30 already tried general output preservation, and R92 disproved static competitive-residual drift as the explanation for its backtracking failures. R107 therefore tests a different, explicit condition established by R106: an observed-point fused native view is a better zero-update readout than either the original prepared state or probability averaging, but the head is fitted only on unfused A/B states. The new third-view constraint preserves the FULL native fusedAB distribution on the current input, not only the residual and not only a presumed-correct winner.

The competing change replaces probability-mean supervision by that same native fusedAB output; a factorial separates these changes. This target may still be wrong. Equal-view LM, physical controls and same-information direct readouts, including direct targets, are needed. A norm-matched old-direction control tests whether any reduction in damage is merely a weaker update. This is a controlled migration of existing ideas, not a novelty or success claim.
