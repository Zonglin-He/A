# R105 evidence and implementation boundaries

OpenScene (CVPR 2023) associates image features with 3D points using camera projection and depth visibility, then averages observed image features per point. The [official mapper](https://github.com/pengsongyou/openscene/blob/main/scripts/feature_fusion/fusion_util.py) checks absolute projected-versus-observed depth difference relative to depth; [official fusion](https://github.com/pengsongyou/openscene/blob/main/scripts/feature_fusion/scannet_openseg.py) counts visible observations before averaging. Its [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.15654) uses learned image-language features and 3D training. Those expert encoders and training are NOT used here, and its task is not this TTA setting.

The transferable prerequisite is physical point/pixel correspondence and explicit visibility, not a guarantee that averaging is semantically correct. R105 retains the existing source-prepared R91 CNN and bidirectional local attention, individually encodes actual RGB observations, and adds sparse RGB banks to canonical A points. It uses stricter raw-cloud surface support and its own sensor registration because the distributed pose provenance has not been established. The .25m nearest-return and .3+.02depth visibility limits are declared engineering assumptions, not OpenScene's published thresholds and not identity certification.

The mean self-teacher and unchanged native-head LM are experimental design choices, not a reproduction of OpenScene. Source A and A+B predictions share one fixed A256 candidate coordinate system; no positive identity labels are inferred from mere agreement. The independent C is a distinct observed image, unlike earlier synthetic C masks, but remains within previously exposed scenes and may be offline/lookahead. No claim of causal online TTA or independent scene testing is permitted.

R12 previously combined sensor registration with external instance masks and visible-surface box correction; that full method was negative. R105 reuses only own sensor algorithms and sensor hash exclusion registries, never mask outputs, accepted instance tracks or task-selected poses. All current registration attempts and failures are retained. Zero wrong-scene geometric acceptances in a finite engineering sample are not a calibrated false-positive rate or semantic identity guarantee.
