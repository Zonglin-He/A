## Postseal descriptive additions

The support-proxy diagnostic is added after all G predictions and task scoring. It counts whether selected observed points fall inside the official target oriented3Dbox, with a fixed0.1m collar sensitivity. This enclosure proxy is not semantic instance membership or pixel-level mask GT: outside points can reflect another object, background, calibration, partial/loose labels or a bad source prior; inside points do not prove literal identity. Different own-support populations are not causal comparisons. The paired summary retains the common-nonempty denominators, losses and ties while still changing pointsets by intervention.
