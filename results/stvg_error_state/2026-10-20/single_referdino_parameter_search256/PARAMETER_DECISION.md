# Parameter decision and improvement coverage

Both datasets retain all128 exposed development parents. The final recommendation follows the declared revision003 policy: original severe-adaptation-loss bounds (HC0, Vid1) first, then maximize parents gaining at least0.1pp within0.1pp of the protected whole-panel mean maximum. All changes below are complete vIoU percentage points. These choices are development selection, not independent confirmation.

HC recommends gamma0.5, lr0.0141421356, tau0.1414213562,40 updates, alpha1.0. The same lr/tau at35 updates has the protected mean maximum29.054387%,54 meaningful improvements and18 meaningful declines. The selected40 updates give29.013530%,60 meaningful improvements and19 meaningful declines. Thus six additional parents gain at least0.1pp, with one additional meaningful decline and a0.040857pp mean concession. Neither setting has an adaptation loss below−5pp. This is a deliberate coverage tradeoff, not a claim that40 updates is best for every parent.

Vid recommends gamma1, lr0.01, tau0.5,30 updates, alpha1.0. Its full vIoU19.764884% is0.044851pp below the protected mean maximum19.809735% (lr0.003/tau0.5/15 updates). The coverage choice has30 meaningful improvements,22 meaningful declines and one severe decline; the mean maximum has16 meaningful improvements,4 meaningful declines and zero severe declines. The original bound allows one severe Vid decline. Accordingly, this recommendation favors wider meaningful gain while retaining substantially more harm than the mean-first alternative. Both results and all signed outcomes are retained; no video-specific switch is used.

Relative to the prior single-expert configuration, HC any-positive coverage rises69/128 to92/128, meaningful coverage34/128 to60/128. Vid any-positive rises60/128 to65/128, meaningful coverage27/128 to30/128. References for these counts are the corresponding own E_gamma+Strong, so initialization changes on HC must be accounted for separately. HC initialization-only gamma calibration contributes+0.633679pp relative to the old E1; its selected subsequent OPD contributes+0.425863pp relative to E_gamma, versus the old+0.299398pp relative to E1. Total HC Full gain over the previous Full is+0.760144pp, not a0.760144pp pure OPD gain.

The actual retained cases prevent an overbroad parameter explanation:

- RC097 changes from−2.444526pp adaptation loss to+1.812089pp. The inspected anchor teacher was correct while the old unobserved prediction moved to a neighboring person. Appropriate initialization/update parameters can improve this propagation outcome.
- RC036 retains an adaptation loss:−3.918074pp relative to its new E_gamma. Its Full is+1.583359pp above the old Full because initialization also changed. The inspected teacher selected another person; parameters do not make that pseudo-label correct.
- BV022 improves+2.716138pp relative to old Full, but remains−1.818258pp below E. The inspected teacher chose the known relation participant rather than the requested child. A softer target can mitigate damage; it does not repair that reference identity.
- BV030 remains−7.116367pp below E in the coverage choice (old−7.158387pp). The inspected old unobserved frame switched to a distant adult. The small-lr/early-stop mean alternative largely avoids this move. This unresolved severe failure is the concrete cost of the wider-coverage recommendation; it is not hidden or claimed fixed.
- BV064 and BV083 retain losses. Their inspected extent mismatch and unobserved identity/propagation problem are not universally removed by tuning.
- BV041 stays near7.25% complete vIoU while its E is already7.28%; this single-expert run does not inherit the old two-expert initialization. It is incorrect to attribute the difference from the much stronger historical detector-based E entirely to OPD's final updates.

Tau changes candidate target probabilities and Adam directions/moments; it is not simply a multiplier on the first Adam update. Learning rate, duration and post-fit alpha affect different parts of the trajectory. Gamma controls initialization before candidates/gradients; alpha controls deterministic output residual after the real fit. Alpha1.0 was selected for both datasets; its search outcomes remain public even though it did not change in the recommendation.

No new parameter grid, additional seed, full-dataset evaluation or model training follows this decision. The Source checkpoints, single ReferDINO, eight observations, Strong and MBR remain fixed.
