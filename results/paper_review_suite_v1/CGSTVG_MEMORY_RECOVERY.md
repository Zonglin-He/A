Sixteen CPU/GPU fixtures and a 512-window fixture were bitwise equal, reducing that fixture's peak allocated bytes from 2,286,396,928 to 1,498,523,136. Actual Source and expert acquisition are now persisted before CPU feedback, preventing a later readout failure from discarding paid evidence.
