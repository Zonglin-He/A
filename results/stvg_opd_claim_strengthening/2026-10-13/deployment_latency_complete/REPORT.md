# Paired Full/Frozen deployment profiling

Fixed 16 original queries per target, two paired repeats. Each application frame cache is empty; OS pages are not evicted. Resident weights per process and saved deterministic subject parses. Source and Full parameters are unchanged. Full calls the real frozen expert wherever the original Native observation scope provides a valid position; all no-information paths remain in the measurements. Frozen keeps only its student model resident. Full complete-round qualification and timed trace-off state/output checks passed.

The complete measured path includes row/cache loading, decoding, student, expert, adaptation, final readout, consolidation and CPU output materialization. Audit-only CPU mathematics, full round serialization and diagnostic extra decoder outputs are excluded and separately qualified. These are local measurements under the stated cache contract; they do not include network access, fresh external language parsing or process startup. No low-latency conclusion is asserted before these actual measurements.

Both methods use the exact qualified O012 native mask/frozen-storage capture. Full stages its inactive expert to CPU only during student capture and restores it before real DINO; these transfers are included in the complete timer. The original cancelled baseline remains stopped with its GPU context resident, and no competing experiment runs. Allocated/reserved peaks describe the measured process, rather than all system GPU residency.

All 64 qualification fits passed full original trace equality, with 4,852,728 trajectory coordinates and 134,208 trace-off output/state coordinates independently checked; qualification made 112 actual DINO calls. All 128 measured paths then passed 383,104 original prediction/state/writeback coordinate comparisons and 896 stage-sum checks. The 64 timed Full paths made 224 DINO calls: 128 HC and 96 Vid. Eight repeated Vid paths had no admitted expert observation and no detector call; four HC and eight Vid repeated Full paths had zero backward steps. They were retained without selection. These counts explain why nominal observation positions and actual expert calls differ.

These results close the original paired deployment profiling phase. They do not close the separate 32-query audited cost study, LN-only streams or any later method/main-experiment decision. No new GT scoring, teacher, method, gate, manuscript or production promotion was introduced.
