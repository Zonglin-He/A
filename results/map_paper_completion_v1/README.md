## Mathematical and cache boundaries

The structural controls share at most one raw Source grid acquisition per missing query. Exactly bound existing raw grids are reused first (384 known shared grids); at most 11,821 new original Source-or-appearance-prefix acquisitions are allowed. No new teacher is required for these full structural controls. Changed geometry requires actual raw-grid pooling; old compact pooled features cannot replace a new ROI pool. The full reference's ROI pool and FullMAP arrays must be bitwise identical on every query. Four actual frozen-cache structural bridges and four temporal bridges have passed repeated exact replay, with zero new model/teacher/GT work.

The corruption study decodes and transforms actual RGB. Its native Source, frame IDs and observation positions must match the original same-view evidence exactly. Compatible old teacher payloads are read only after exact RGB/context/payload/receipt SHA checks; misses write only to this new namespace. A paid Source payload and SHA are checkpointed **before** teacher acquisition. Prediction, lossless sufficient compact state, cold replay/readback and receipt must commit before disposing of only this study's completed NEW raw scratch. Uncommitted paid raw, attempts and failures remain intact. Old final Full arrays are only an upstream compatibility check, not the new current-MAP result or a new performance comparator.

Temporal controls reuse sufficient complete-set inputs without model, RGB, teacher or optimizer work. TTA uses exact cached native decoder contexts with real forward/backward updates; it does not collect new Source or teacher outputs. Its zero new acquisition count does not mean zero deployment acquisition cost. FullMAP uses eight expert observations and no optimizer; Tent/SAR use zero expert observations and five update iterations. Both budgets and the adapter's scope are disclosed.

Deployment timing disables cross-query RGB/visual/Source/evidence/teacher caches, includes actual decode, parsing, transfers, Source, expert, geometry, auxiliary DP, real pooling, one prototype update, joint-MAP and final DP, and verifies the outputs outside the timer. Models use the original resident/offload schedule. Startup/warmup and nested component durations are separate; nested durations must not be added to outer total. Results are conditional on the measured shared host, not a throughput claim. All192calls and repeat-averaged per-parent paired intervals are now exported in [actual deployment timing](deployment/ANALYSIS.md).
